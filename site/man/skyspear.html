skyspear(6)

The manual page that comes home with skyspear v1.0.0.

$ tty1 man skyspear

NAME

skyspear — a prehistoric lost world in the terminal

SYNOPSIS

skyspear [-mouse=bool] [-mute] [-seed n] [-shot file.png] [-world folder] [-worlds folder]

skyspear -version

DESCRIPTION

skyspear is a block-building game of a prehistoric lost world, drawn in the terminal. You are a cave person among dinosaurs, fire mountains and falling stars, and you begin with nothing. Each night a comet grows among the stars, and on the twelfth it arrives. Make fire, hunt or hide, paint what you have met, and win the trust of a camp until its people come to live at your fire. The art and the sounds are made by code.

A world has no end and is made from a seed. It is played in one of two ways, chosen when it is made:

survival
You dig what you need, carry it, eat and stay alive. The first night is quiet; from the second, hunters come in the dark.
creative
Every block is at hand, and nothing hurts you.

The title lists the worlds, the last played first. Choose one to play it or to delete it, or make a new one: it asks for a name, a seed, and how it is played. A world is saved when you leave it, and also on Ctrl-C or when the window closes.

Use a terminal that reports key releases, such as Ghostty, kitty, WezTerm, foot, iTerm2 or Alacritty: there you can walk and turn at once. In other terminals only one key stays held at a time, and the game says so. Every cell is two pixels, so a bigger window is a sharper picture, and true color looks best.

The options are as follows:

-mouse=bool
With false, leave the pointer to the terminal, to select text. The default is true: the mouse aims, breaks and places.
-mute
Play with no sound, and leave the sound device alone. A machine with no sound device plays in silence too.
-seed n
The number a new world is made from, offered as its seed. The same number makes the same world. 0, the default, takes one from the clock.
-shot file.png
Draw one frame of play into a PNG and quit, without a terminal.
-world folder
Play only the world in folder, with no list.
-worlds folder
List the worlds in folder in place of the usual one; see FILES.
-version
Print the version and quit.

KEYS

W A S D
Walk and strafe. In a galewing's glide, W dives and A and D turn.
arrow keys
Look.
Space
Jump, swim up, or rise when flying. On a beast you ride, jump or charge.
X
Sink when flying.
F
Flying on and off, in creative.
C
Crouch, and stand again: slow, low and quiet. On a beast you ride, get down; in a glide, let go.
V
See through your eyes, or from behind your shoulder.
L
The next of sixteen looks for your body.
Q
Break the block aimed at, or hit the creature aimed at; held, it goes on.
E
Place the block aimed at, and use what is aimed at with what is in your hand: eat, feed a young beast, get on a beast of yours, paint a face of rock or read a painting, open a clay pot or a hearth, lie down on a fur bed, or light torches at a fire. Held with a fire drill on a hearth, it lights the hearth. Pressed on a person, it shows them what you hold, to trade; crouched, it gives it.
R
Throw the spear in your hand, or with a sling a pebble; held, it goes on.
1 to 9, Tab
Pick a slot of the hotbar, or the next one.
I
Your things: move them between the hotbar and the pack, and make things from them.
T
Move the clock on six hours.
F3
Frame numbers, the seed, the day and the hour, and the days until the comet arrives.
Esc
The pause menu, with these keys on it; on a menu, back.
Ctrl-C
Save and quit.

Letters work in either case.

MOUSE

Whichever was used last aims: move or click the mouse and the pointer aims, press an arrow key and the middle of the view aims again.

left button
Break, or hit; held still, it goes on. Held and dragged, it turns the view, a cell at a time.
right button
Place.
wheel
Pick a slot of the hotbar.

A click on a slot of the hotbar picks it, a click in the bag takes or puts down what is in a slot, and a click on a menu chooses a line.

ENVIRONMENT

COLORTERM
truecolor or 24bit draws in true color. Inside tmux or over ssh, set it if the terminal can show true color.
TERM
Read with COLORTERM to choose the colors.
HOME
Where the config folder is, on macOS and on Linux.
XDG_CONFIG_HOME
On Linux, the config folder, in place of ~/.config.

FILES

The config folder is ~/Library/Application Support on macOS and ~/.config on Linux.

skyspear/worlds/
The worlds, one folder each, named as the world was named. Each holds world.json and one small file for each chunk that was changed; the rest of a world is made again from its seed.
skyspear/settings.json
The settings: mouse speed, view distance, field of view, volume, whether the view shakes, and the view last chosen.

EXIT STATUS

The skyspear utility exits 0 on success, and >0 if an error occurs.
