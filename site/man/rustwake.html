rustwake(6)

The manual page that comes home with rustwake v0.1.0.

$ tty1 man rustwake

NAME

rustwake — a first-person shooter in the terminal

SYNOPSIS

rustwake [-level n] [-version]

DESCRIPTION

rustwake is a first-person shooter, drawn in the terminal. Clear the rogue machines out of a dark plant, one map at a time. Every picture in it is drawn by code.

Find the exit on each map: a green lamp on a steel post. Health packs and ammo lie about. You start with 100 health and 20 rounds, and keep what you have from one map to the next. A health pack gives back 25, and is left where it lies while your health is full; ammo gives 10 rounds. A shot does one point of damage and spends one round, so aim.

The machines are these:

Crawler
Walks at you and bites.
Sentry
Stands back and fires bolts.
Drone
Fast, floats, and falls to one hit.
Hauler
Slow, takes eight hits, and hits hard.
Overseer
The boss of the last map. It takes forty hits, and fires three bolts at once.

The 12 maps come in three zones of four, each with its own walls, floors and ceilings:

  1. Pump Hall, Filter Beds, Pipe Gallery, Sluice Gate.
  2. Slag Run, Casting Floor, Ore Lift, Furnace Row.
  3. Cable Trench, Relay Hall, Cold Core, Control.

If your health runs out, the screen cracks and runs red. After a moment, Enter or Space cuts the scene short. Then choose Retry, to play the map again with 100 health and 20 rounds, or Quit. Clear the last map and the plant is cleared: Play again starts on the first map.

The menus are worked with Up, Down and Enter, which chooses on a new press only. Nothing is saved: each run starts on the first map, or on the one -level names.

The game needs a terminal that shows true color. 80 columns by 24 rows works, and 160 columns or more shows the large title. To walk and turn at the same time, use a terminal that reports key releases, such as Ghostty, kitty or WezTerm. In others only one key is held at a time.

The options are as follows:

-level n
Start on map n, from 1 to 12. The default is 1.
-version
Print the version and quit.

KEYS

W, S
Walk forward, and back.
A, D
Strafe left, and right.
Left, Right, or Q, E
Turn.
Space
Fire. Held, it fires again as fast as the gun allows.
Esc
Pause. On the pause menu, resume.
Ctrl-C
Quit at once.

Letters work in either case.

ENVIRONMENT

rustwake reads no environment variables of its own. The glonk engine, which draws it, reads these:

COLORTERM
‘truecolor’ or ‘24bit’ says the terminal shows true color, and colors are sent exactly. Without it, or a TERM that says so, each color is sent as the nearest one of the 256-color palette. Inside tmux or over ssh it may not come through; set it where the terminal does show true color.
TERM
A name with ‘truecolor’ or ‘24bit’ in it, or ending in ‘-direct’, says the same.
TERM_PROGRAM
‘Apple_Terminal’ is the Terminal of macOS, which leaves a thin line along the edge of half-block characters. There the picture is drawn in whole cells, a little blockier.

EXIT STATUS

The rustwake utility exits 0 on success, and >0 if an error occurs. It exits 2 when an option is wrong, or -level names no map.

SEE ALSO

The README, at https://github.com/usrgames/rustwake, says how the game is made. glonk, the engine that draws it, is at https://glonk.dev.
