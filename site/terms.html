House rules.

The games are free

Every game here is free to play, in the page or at home.

What you may do with a game

The games belong to KOHANTIC LLC. Download one from this site or from its release on GitHub, install it and play it. Do not sell it, share copies of it anywhere else, or change it. The licence that comes with each release is the last word.

The code that is open

Three things here are open source, under the MIT licence: the code of this site, tty1, and glonk, the engine the games are built on. The games' code is not.

As it is

We offer the games and this site as they are. We make no promise that they are free of faults, or that they will always be here. Each licence says the same at more length.

The relay

The relay is there so two people can play a match. Use it for that. Do not flood it, pick at it, or use it to carry anything else. We may limit or block traffic that harms it, and we may change it or switch it off.

Who runs this
