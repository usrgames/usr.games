What we keep.

Almost nothing. There are no accounts here, no cookies and no ads.

On these pages

These pages set no cookies and keep nothing in your browser. Cloudflare serves them, and sees each request the way any host does: your address, your browser and the page you asked for.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare adds one small script to each page for it. It tells us how many people came, which pages they opened, which site sent them, their country, what kind of browser and device they used, and how fast the page loaded. It uses no cookies, and Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint anyone.

A game you play on its page is downloaded from this site and runs in your browser. It saves nothing there: what you do in it is gone when you leave the page.

In a match over the net

When two people play over the net, their games talk through our relay. It holds a match in memory while it is played: a room, two seats, and the moves on their way from one seat to the other. A move is dropped once it is delivered. A seat that goes quiet for 45 seconds is cleared, and a room nobody joins ends after 10 minutes.

The relay asks for no name, writes nothing to disk and keeps no log of who played. DigitalOcean hosts it, and sees the addresses that connect to it.

Outside a match over the net, the games make no network connections at all.

Taking a game home

The installer comes from this site and downloads tty1 from GitHub. tty1 asks this site for the list of games, and downloads each game from GitHub. Installing with Go goes through Go's module proxy, which Google runs. Requests to GitHub and Google go to them, under their own policies.
